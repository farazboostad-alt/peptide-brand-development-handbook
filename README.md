
# Peptide Brand Development Handbook

This repository shares practical ideas and planning resources for developing brands in the peptide industry.

## Main Areas

- Brand identity development
- Market positioning
- Customer communication
- Content planning
- Business growth strategies

## Building a Strong Brand

### Brand Identity
Create a clear identity that reflects the business and its values.

### Market Positioning
Understand the market and identify what makes the brand different.

### Communication
Use clear and consistent messaging across digital channels.

### Content Planning
Develop useful content that answers audience questions and explains the business.
